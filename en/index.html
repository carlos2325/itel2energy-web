Who we are

Independent energy agents

ITEL2 ENERGY is not a large trading house: we are two people working as agents. We open and connect energy and derivatives deals between buyers and sellers, and we find the financing that makes them possible. Our value lies in the relationship, the judgment and the ability to close, not in size.

How we work

Close, direct and closing-focused

  • We put buyers and sellers of energy and derivative products in touch.
  • We negotiate terms and accompany the deal through to agreement.
  • We find financing with banks, institutions and investors for deals and projects.
  • Direct dealing with whoever decides and executes, with no unnecessary layers.

Financing search

The other half of what we do: when a deal or an energy project needs capital, we put it in front of whoever can finance it and help negotiate the agreement.

How we help

We put the deal in front of the right capital

We act as a bridge between whoever has the deal (operators, producers, buyers) and whoever can finance it (banks, institutions and investors). We are not a fund or a bank: we open the door, present the case and accompany the negotiation through to close, drawing on the same relationships from the energy business.
