Who we are

Independent energy agents

ITEL2 ENERGY is not a large trading house: we are a team working as agents. We open and connect energy and derivatives deals between buyers and sellers, and we find the financing that makes them possible. Our value lies in the relationship, the judgment and the ability to close, not in size.

How we work

Close, direct and closing-focused

  • We put buyers and sellers of energy and derivative products in touch.
  • We negotiate terms and accompany the deal through to agreement.
  • We find financing for energy infrastructure investment projects in Europe, or find projects for financiers (mandate).
  • Direct dealing with whoever decides and executes, with no unnecessary layers.

Two activities, one style: open the deal and carry it through to close. We work deal by deal, with judgment and direct dealing.

How we help
