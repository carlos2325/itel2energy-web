Banks · Institutions · Investors

We put the deal in front of whoever can finance it.

When an energy deal or a project needs capital, we put it in front of banks, institutions and investors in our network and accompany the negotiation. We are not a bank or a fund: we open the door, present the case and help close it, drawing on the same relationships from the energy business.

How we help finance

Depending on the deal, we find and facilitate the right instrument with the financiers in our network. We don't issue them ourselves: we manage them with whoever is responsible.

01

Trade finance

Documentary and standby letters of credit (L/C), guarantees, prepayments and inventory financing to cover the commercial cycle between purchase, transport and collection.

02

Structured commodity finance

Structures backed by physical flow and contracts: pre-export finance, borrowing base and collateral against product, shipments or receivables.

03

Energy project finance

Debt and equity structuring for energy projects: financial modeling, securing sources, syndication and support through to financial close.

04

Origination & offtake

Design of long-term supply and offtake agreements that provide revenue predictability and give the project bankability.

05

Transaction structuring

Deal design, commercial due diligence, selection and management of counterparties and documentary coordination between the parties.

06

Risk management & compliance

Price and currency hedging, KYC/AML, sanctions screening and end-to-end documentary traceability.
